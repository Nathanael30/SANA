# SANA Demo Script

**Scenario Name:** High-Risk Preeclampsia Detection

## Setup
1. Ensure both the FastAPI backend and React frontend are running.
2. Ensure Ollama is running locally with the target model (e.g., `gemma:2b`).
3. Open the browser to `http://localhost:5173`.

## The Demo

### 1. Introduction
"Hello, this is SANA, an offline-first AI co-pilot for Community Health Workers. We are going to assess a patient who has come to the rural clinic."

### 2. Enter Patient Data
- **Patient Name:** Maria
- **Age:** 26
- **Gestational Week:** 32
- **Blood Pressure:** 150 Sys / 100 Dia
- **Symptoms:** Check `headache`, `swelling/edema`, and `blurred vision`.
- **Observation Notes:** "Patient appears very pale and complains of severe headache since yesterday morning."

### 3. Assessment
- Click the **"Assess Risk"** button.
- Wait a few seconds for the local LLM to generate the assessment.

### 4. Results
- Point out the **Risk Level Badge**: It should say **HIGH RISK** or **EMERGENCY** (based on the rules triggering for high BP + symptoms past 20 weeks).
- Show the **Clinical Summary** generated by the AI explaining why this is high risk (suspicion of preeclampsia).
- Note the **Warning Signs** identified.
- Note the **Recommended Action**.
- Highlight the **Referral Letter** that was automatically drafted.

### 5. Save Visit
- Click **"Save Visit"**.
- Navigate to the **"Logs"** tab to show that Maria's visit is now securely stored locally on the device in SQLite.
