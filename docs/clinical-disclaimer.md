# Clinical Disclaimer

**IMPORTANT: FOR DEMONSTRATION AND EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY**

SANA is a hackathon prototype designed to demonstrate the potential of offline-first artificial intelligence in assisting Community Health Workers (CHWs) in low-resource settings.

1. **Not a Medical Device:** This software is not a certified medical device. It has not been approved by the FDA, WHO, or any other regulatory body.
2. **No Diagnosis:** SANA does not provide medical diagnoses, professional medical advice, or treatment plans.
3. **Accuracy:** The AI models and clinical rule engines used in this prototype may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate outputs ("hallucinations").
4. **Emergency Protocols:** In real-world scenarios, CHWs must always follow local emergency clinical guidelines, WHO protocols, and refer patients to qualified healthcare professionals immediately when danger signs are present.

By using this software, you acknowledge that it is a proof-of-concept and assume all responsibility for its use.
